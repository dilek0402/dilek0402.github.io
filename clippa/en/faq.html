Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask most about Clippa.

What is Clippa?

Clippa is a lightweight clipboard manager for macOS. It stores the text, images and files you copy in the menu bar and lets you recall them in seconds.

How do I open Clippa?

Press ⇧⌘C (Shift+Command+C) to open the clipboard history. Navigate with the arrow keys and paste with Enter. ⌘1…⌘9 paste the first nine items.

Is Clippa free?

Clippa is a one-time paid app on the Mac App Store. There are no ads and no subscription.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your clipboard history is kept only on your Mac; it is never sent to the cloud, no account is required, and there is no tracking.

Are my passwords saved to the history?

No. Clippa automatically detects sensitive content from password managers and does not store it in the history.

Which macOS version is required?

Clippa runs on macOS 14.0 and later.

Does it store only text?

No. In addition to text, it keeps images and files in your clipboard history.
