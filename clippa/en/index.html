Your clipboard, remembered

Clippa is a fast, keyboard-first clipboard manager for macOS. It quietly saves the text, images and files you copy and brings them back in seconds — right from the menu bar.

Features FAQ Privacy
🕑

Clipboard History

Everything you copy accumulates quietly in the menu bar, ready to recall.

🔎

Instant Search

Find any past copy with full-text, fuzzy or regex matching.

⌨️

Keyboard-First

⇧⌘C to open, arrow keys to navigate, Enter to paste. Never touch the mouse.

⚡

Quick Shortcuts

⌘1…⌘9 paste your first nine items instantly. Pin the ones you reuse.

🖼️

Text, Images & Files

Not just text — Clippa keeps images and files in your history too.

🔒

Private by Design

No cloud, no account, no tracking. Sensitive password-manager content is filtered automatically.

Why Clippa?

Lightweight and out of your way

  • Lives silently in the menu bar — no dock icon, no clutter
  • Tiny footprint, instant to open
  • Everything stays on your Mac — nothing is collected or transmitted
  • Family Sharing supported (up to six family members)
