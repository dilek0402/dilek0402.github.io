Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask most about BoxKeeper.

What is BoxKeeper?

BoxKeeper is a home inventory app for moving and storage. You photograph each item, assign it to a box, and find it in seconds when you search. It works fully offline.

Does it work offline? Where is my data stored?

Yes, it is fully offline. All of your items, boxes and photos are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server. Every feature works even in airplane mode.

Is BoxKeeper free?

Yes. The app shows ads that do not use your personal data; your inventory is never shared with advertisers.

How do I add items?

Take a photo of the item with the camera, give it a name and assign it to a box or location. You can add an item in under 10 seconds.

How do QR labels work?

You print a QR label for each box and stick it on. Scanning it with your phone instantly opens that box's contents.

What happens when I move a box?

When you move a box to a new room or location, every item inside it updates automatically.

Can I import my existing list from Excel or CSV?

Yes. You can import your existing item list from an Excel/CSV file in seconds.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. You can back up your entire inventory, photos included, as a single file and restore it anytime.

Which devices does it run on?

BoxKeeper runs on iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 and later.
