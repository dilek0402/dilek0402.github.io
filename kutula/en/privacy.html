Privacy Policy

BoxKeeper is built privacy-first.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Summary

BoxKeeper is an offline app for tracking your belongings and boxes. The inventory you create — items, photos, boxes and locations — is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers or any third party. No account is required.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data for the core functionality. Everything you enter stays on your device:

  • Item and box information: names, notes, categories, locations — on your device.
  • Photos: the photos you take or select are kept in your device's file system and are never uploaded.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you choose to photograph an item. Captured images stay on your device. When you pick from your photo library, only the photos you select are accessed.

Advertising

To be offered for free, BoxKeeper shows ads through Google AdMob. The ad framework may use some device-level technical information (e.g. an advertising identifier, approximate location, device type) to serve ads. Your inventory data (items, photos, boxes) is never shared with ad networks.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent screen (Google UMP) on first launch, where you choose whether to allow personalized ads. On iOS, an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is also shown; if you decline, no tracking takes place. For Google's ad data practices see policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use in-app analytics that track your behavior. Apart from ad consent, we do not build a profile that identifies you.

Data sharing

We never share, sell or rent your inventory data. Your backup file is saved only by you, to a location you choose (e.g. iCloud Drive, Files).

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated; we will update the date on this page for material changes.
