## 3. Product Hunt lansmanı

- **Tagline (60 karakter):**
  `Personal finance for Turkey — budget, assets & real returns`
- **İsim:** Parametre
- **Kısa açıklama:**
  Track your budget, assets (gold, FX, deposits, stocks) with live prices, and debts — all in one privacy-first iOS app. See your real, inflation-adjusted returns using official CPI data. Built for Turkey (Central Bank rates, local taxes) but works anywhere.
- **İlk yorum (maker comment):**
  > Hi Product Hunt! 👋 I built Parametre because I couldn't find a finance app that understood the Turkish context — high inflation, gold as savings, Central Bank FX rates, and local loan taxes (KKDF/BSMV). It tracks your budget, all your assets with live prices, and debts, and it shows your **real return after inflation** using official CPI/PPI — not just the nominal number. Everything stays on your device with end-to-end encrypted iCloud backup. Would love your feedback! 🙏

**İpucu:** Salı–Perşembe, saat 00:01 PST (TSİ ~11:00) yayınla. Önceden "coming soon" sayfası aç. Lansman günü çevreni yorum/oy için uyar.
